ABOUT — 5 PROMISES

It started with what
we wouldn't do.

This project began with three refusals: "We won't walk beside you," "We won't support you," and "We won't integrate."

So what will we do? Here are five promises.

Five Promises
[ 05 / 05 ]

Three refusals and five promises. The promises outnumber the refusals.

01
Understand the needs
What is needed and what is not. As someone who has been there, we research and verify. We don't add features based on guesswork.
— LISTEN
02
Protect user autonomy
Decisions belong to the user. We only hand over information. We won't take your pace, your choices, or your self-assessment.
— RESPECT
03
Design for everyone
Neurodiversity is the norm, not the exception. Readability and usability come first.
— ACCESSIBILITY
04
Make it public
Aggregation formulas, tag definitions, design philosophy, source code. We show the reasoning behind every decision. Verifiability is transparency.
— OPEN
05
Stay independent
Local first. No cloud dependency. Your data stays on your device. No subscription fatigue.
— LOCAL FIRST
