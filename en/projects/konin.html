How It Works
[ TRANSPARENT FORMULA ]

The frequency formula
is public.

Calculated as a weighted sum that gives more weight to recent exams. Weights are fixed values. The most recent session is 1.0, with earlier sessions decaying step by step.

Aggregation targets and tag definitions are all public. Verifiability is transparency.

— RECENT WEIGHTED SCORE
∑(appearances × session_weight)
Latest session× 1.0
1 session ago× 0.8
2 sessions ago× 0.6
3 sessions ago× 0.4
Older× 0.2
